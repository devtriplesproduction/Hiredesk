"DO NOT DO" RULES
Do not interview candidates before basic screening.
Do not repeatedly ask candidates for information they have already provided.
Do not promise selection before the complete process.
Do not change the hiring flow randomly.
Do not send different information about salary, location, work mode, or eligibility from the approved job description.
Keep candidate communication professional and concise.
